# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers who run several local terminal and AI-agent sessions at once. They use purplemux to monitor work, switch context quickly, and keep active tasks available across app restarts and devices.

## Product Purpose

purplemux is a local-first workspace for persistent terminal, Claude Code, and Codex sessions. It keeps session state, project context, and supporting tools in one place so developers can coordinate parallel work without losing flow.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, dense, dependable, and keyboard-first. The interface should feel familiar to users of professional development tools and stay out of the way of the work.

## Anti-references

- Consumer productivity dashboards that add decorative cards, gamification, or excessive empty space.
- Notion-style databases and page hierarchies where a direct editor or terminal surface is enough.
- Rich-text editors that hide Markdown semantics or replace standard text-editing behavior.
- Custom controls that behave differently from familiar desktop development tools.

## Design Principles

- Preserve flow: common actions stay one shortcut or one direct control away.
- Keep work recoverable: app restarts must not discard active workspace state.
- Prefer familiar development-tool conventions over novel interaction patterns.
- Keep keyboard and pointer paths equivalent and clearly discoverable.
- Store user content locally and make destructive boundaries explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast. Every action must be keyboard accessible with visible focus, semantic labels, and predictable focus order. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never rely on color alone to communicate state.
